**1. Provo City Housing Authority (provohousing.org)**
* Accepting applications for housing assistance. There are separate waiting lists for

* apply for housing, but you do have to live in Provo City to receive assistance through
* the Provo Housing Authority.
* Public Housing: owned and managed by the Provo Housing Authority within the
* city of Provo, including housing for families, seniors 55 and older, and disabled
* citizens. Housing Choice Voucher Program: Tenants choose their own housing
* in Provo City - house, apartment, townhouse, etc., rather than a public housing
* unit. The housing authority pays the landlord a subsidy directly and the tenant
* covers the difference. These are called vouchers for Section 8 housing.
* Supportive Housing: for special populations who need supportive services
* from the community in their home. Participation in this program requires a referral from one of the partnering organizations such as Wasatch Mental Health
* or the Center for Women and Children in Crisis.
* Senior Housing: Affordable housing for seniors 55+. One bedroom and studio apartments with onsite laundry facilities, secure entry, community rooms and more. ADA units are available.
* Shelter Plus Care Vouchers: For the chronically homeless with disabilities who have been on the streets over one year or have had three instances of homelessness over 5 years. These vouchers are designed for people with serious mental illness, chronic substance abuse, etc. They must agree to case management through partners like Wasatch Mental Health. Vouchers are for
* Section 8 housing.
* Family Self - Sufficiency Program: Voluntary and free program designed to
* help voucher program or section 8 rental assistance recipients to become self sufficient by helping them set goals to overcome barriers such as employment,
* financial capability, or education. See Provo Housing Authority - Self
* sufficiency Program flyer for more detailed information.
